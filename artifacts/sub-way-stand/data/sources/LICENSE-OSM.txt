line2-jamsil-osm.json preserves the Line 2 railway ways and their nodes,
including source element ids, versions, timestamps and tags. Unrelated
features from the bounded extract were removed. This is an offline import;
the deployed application does not query the OSM editing API.

Reproduce preprocessing (Node 24, from the app directory):
node scripts/import-line2-centerline.mts

The importer follows connected railway nodes between named stop_position
nodes at Jamsil and Jamsillaru. It projects into a local metric east/north
plane and computes signed curvature at 20 m samples using an 80 m baseline.
The median curvature of each contiguous same-turn run represents that run.
The reverse direction reuses the representative alignment with reversed
offsets and curve signs; the precise directional rail is not verified.

Mapped coordinates, cant and actual rail use have not been surveyed or
validated against a real journey. The 10 m coordinate perturbation is an
assumed sensitivity scenario, not a measured positional accuracy or a
probabilistic confidence bound. Imported curves remain low confidence,
osm-centerline, and ineligible for live directional voice guidance.
